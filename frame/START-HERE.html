UFO frame: at Clayt's, start here

Saturday 17 Oct 2026. Today: cut all 20 bars of 2020 into the 70 pieces. Assembly happens at home. The full cut sheet is the next file; this page is what to bring, what to watch, and the order.

Bring

  • All 20 bars (two 10-packs of 1220 mm 2020). All 20 get used: there is no spare bar.
  • The cut sheet (file 1) on the phone, or printed
  • Metric tape measure, fine marker, masking tape (labels A1 … X4)
  • Deburring tool or a file, for every cut end
  • Safety glasses, hearing protection, gloves for handling (chips are sharp)
  • 2 clamps and a block for the stop block, if Clayt's saw has no stop
  • A bag or box per letter, so the pieces don't mix
  • Optional, if you'll tap there too: cordless drill, the M6 drill-and-tap bit, WD-40. Tapping can wait for home.
The saw blade. Aluminium needs a carbide non-ferrous blade (high tooth count, 80T+ on a 10 in saw). A wood blade grabs and throws the bar. Clamp the bar on both sides of the blade, feed slowly, a little wax or WD-40 on the blade, and let it stop before lifting.

Order at the saw

  1. Set the stop block for 900. Cut one A, measure it (900 mm, 35⅜ in), then cut the rest: 8 A from bars 1–8, 8 B from bars 9–16. Keep the two offcut piles apart (1–8 and 9–16). Each is ~317 mm.
  2. Stop at 300. 8 C (hub ring) from the bars 1–8 offcuts.
  3. Stop at 137.8, then 142. From each bars 9–16 offcut: one D (core ring), then one E (hub post).
  4. Bar 17: 5 G 95, 1 X 400, 2 L 150.
  5. Bar 18: 3 G 95, 4 F 90, 8 K 60. Short pieces: keep fingers clear and clamp, or use a backing block.
  6. Bar 19: 2 H 373, then 1 X 400 (see the note below).
  7. Bar 20: 2 X 400, 2 L 150.

The rule: one stop-block setting per letter, cut every piece of that letter before moving it, and check the first one with the tape. Pieces of one letter matching each other matters more than the exact number. Label each piece as it comes off, and deburr it straight away.

H box rails: cut them at 373. FRAME-2020 says to wait and cut H to "the inside width, about 333". That line is stale: the rails lie on top of the core ring, spanning it outside to outside, and with D at 137.8 that width is 137.8 + 16.6 = 154.4 mm per side × 2.414 = 372.7 mm. So 373, as on the cut sheet, with the holes 10 mm in from each end. If you'd rather be sure, cut bar 19's X first and bring the rest home; then H waits for a saw.
Check a bar's length first. "48 in" bars are often 1219 mm, and some run a little short. The plan leaves 14–108 mm per bar, so you're fine unless a bar is more than ~14 mm short. The tightest are bars 1–8 (900 + C 300 leaves 14 mm). Measure one before the first cut.
