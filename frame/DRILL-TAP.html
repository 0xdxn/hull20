Station 1 · Tap 88 ends

  1. Bit in the drill, clutch on a low setting so it slips before the tap snaps. Low speed.
  2. A squirt of WD-40 in the centre hole. Keep the drill square to the end; let the bit pull itself in.
  3. Go 25 mm deep (a strip of tape on the bit marks it), then back it out in reverse.
  4. Blow the chips out. Run an M6 screw in by hand on the first one of each letter to check the thread.

Station 2 · Gang-mark

  1. Lay all pieces of one letter side by side, the reference ends (IN for B, the dot for D) pushed against one straight block. Clamp them.
  2. Measure once on the outside piece, then square a line across every piece at once. Same line on every piece, so the holes match even if the tape is off by a hair.
  3. Mark the face you'll drill through with an X, so a hole never ends up on the wrong side.

Corners · the 135° brackets (J1 rim, J6 hub ring, J7 core ring)

24 corners, one bracket each, two M5 holes per bracket: 48 holes, 5.5 mm. Same job on every corner.

  1. Lay the ring flat on the floor, pieces in order. Push each pair of ends together until the inner corners touch; the small open wedge is on the outside.
  2. Hold the bracket flat on the inner faces, its bend tight into the corner, centred top to bottom (2 mm of bar showing above and below). A spring clamp holds it.
  3. Mark both holes through the bracket with a fine marker or a 5 mm transfer punch. Take the bracket off.
  4. Drill 5.5 mm on each mark with the drill guide on the inner face, straight through to the outside face.
  5. Countersink each hole on the outside face until an M5 flat-head sits flush (the skin runs over it).
  6. Bolt: M5 × 35 flat-head in from outside, through the bar and the bracket, nylock on the bracket side. Snug both bolts, check the corner is tight, then tighten. Vinyl cap over each nylock.
Faster, if the first corner checks out. Do one corner by marking through the bracket, then measure from each bar's end to its mark. If both legs read the same (about 45 mm), gang-mark that distance on both ends of every A, C and D piece, on the inner face, and drill all 48 today with the rest. On D (137.8 long) the 45 mm holes miss the centre hole (68.9) and the 29 mm plate-leg hole, which goes through a different face.
